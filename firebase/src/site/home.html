Three steps to a finished quote

Pick the services

Choose what the job needs, such as a patio, a new lawn and a fence, then add your customer's details.

Type in the sizes

Lengths, widths and depths, row by row. The running total updates as you go, with every material and labour day priced.

Send it

Check the letter, then email or WhatsApp it to the customer. When they say yes, the payment plan and calendar are done for you.

Win the job, and the rest sorts itself out

Questions

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. Create an account and use everything for 7 days. If you don't choose a plan, your quotes stay safe and readable; you just can't create new ones until you subscribe.

How do I cancel?

Any time, from Account → Manage subscription. You won't be charged again, and you keep access until the end of the month or year you've already paid for. Your data is kept for 12 months in case you come back.

Is it only for landscapers?

It's built around landscaping, with the services a landscaper prices every week ready to go. The service builder lets any trade add its own services, materials, quantities and quote wording.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Quotes open and save without signal, and sync when you're back online.

Where is my data kept?

On Google's servers in London. Only your business can see it, and you can download all of it, or delete your account, at any time.

Can I use my own logo and wording?

Yes. Upload your logo, pick your colour, and change any wording on the quote letter, either for every quote or just for one.
