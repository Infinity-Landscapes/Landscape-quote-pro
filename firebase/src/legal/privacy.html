Your customers' details: the information you put into your quotes and jobs about your own customers (their names, addresses, emails, phone numbers and job details) belongs to your business. 2. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To provide the Service: creating and securing your account, storing your data, applying your plan and device limit, and taking payment. Lawful basis: performing our contract with your business.
  • To send you service messages: account emails (such as password resets), receipts, and notices about your subscription, price changes or changes to our terms. Lawful basis: our contract, and our legitimate interest in running the Service.
  • To keep the Service safe and working, and to improve it: preventing fraud and misuse, fixing problems, and understanding how features are used. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests.
  • To meet our legal obligations: keeping financial and tax records, and responding to lawful requests. Lawful basis: legal obligation.

We don't sell your information, and we don't send marketing emails. If we ever want to, we'll ask first or give you a simple way to opt out.

4. Transfers outside the UK

Your business data is stored in the United Kingdom. Your sign-in details are held by Google's sign-in service, which may store them outside the UK, and some of our other providers may access or process information outside the UK, for example in the USA. Where they do, they use the safeguards the law requires, such as the UK's adequacy regulations, the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or the UK's approved international data transfer agreement or addendum.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account and business data: while your subscription or trial is active, and for 12 months after it ends so you can return and carry on. Then it is deleted. If you delete your account, your business data and login are deleted straight away.
  • Billing records: for 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Support emails: for up to 2 years after your query is resolved.

6. Cookies and storage on your device

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. The app stores some information in your browser so it can work: your sign-in, a random identifier for your device (for the device limit), a copy of your data so it works offline, and your preferences. These are strictly necessary for the Service, so we don't ask for consent for them. Stripe's payment pages use their own cookies to process payments and prevent fraud; see Stripe's privacy policy.

7. Keeping it safe

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting provider. Each business can only reach its own data, enforced on our servers, and our own administrative access is limited to what we need to run and support the Service. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the ICO where the law requires.

8. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal information; to have it corrected; to have it deleted; to restrict or object to how we use it; and to receive it in a reusable format. Much of this you can do yourself in the app: Account → Download all my data, and Account → Delete my account. For anything else, email support@landscapequotepro.co.uk. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Children

The Service is for businesses and isn't intended for anyone under 18.
