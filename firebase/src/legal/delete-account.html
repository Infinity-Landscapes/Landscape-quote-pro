Delete your account

You can delete your account and all its data yourself in the app: tap the person icon at the top right, then Delete my account. You'll be asked to type DELETE and your password.

This permanently removes your business settings, quotes, jobs, calendar, logo and login. If you subscribe through the App Store or Google Play, cancel the subscription there too.

Can't get into the app? Email support@landscapequotepro.co.uk from the address you sign in with and we'll delete it for you within 30 days.
